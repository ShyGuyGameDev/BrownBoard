# Dart (AI work management)

Identity check, as of 27 October 2026: this is **Dart / Dart AI**, the AI project-and-agent workspace from **Keeple, Inc.** (YC W22). Current product sites are [dartai.com](https://www.dartai.com/) and [itsdart.com](https://itsdart.com/), which serve the same homepage (“The workplace for the agent era”). It is not the Dart programming language and not Flutter. It has not been renamed away from Dart, acquired, or shut down. Positioning has shifted since the February 2024 launch from “AI that does PM chores” to a workplace where people and software agents share tasks.

Do not mix this product with [dart.ceo](https://dart.ceo/) (“Dart: AI Company Brain”), a different offering (flat $199/month leadership brief, Slack/Gmail/CRM). That site is not Keeple’s PM product.

## What is Dart today, which views does it offer, and which integrations does it have?

### Takeaway
Dart is an AI-native project manager that creates and grooms structured tasks from chat, forwarded email, and explicit Slack actions, and that now also assigns work to built-in or connected agents. Confirmed layouts are list, kanban board, calendar, and roadmap, plus docs and sprints. There is no evidenced freeform drawing canvas and no evidenced meeting-transcription product.

A lead agent breaks a goal into a plan and coordinates specialist agents; people set direction and step in for judgment. The product shows Chat mode (plan, produce a spec) and Build mode (execute from a linked task). ### Inferences
- The honest 2026 description is “AI project system plus an agent workplace,” not “a bot that silently turns every meeting and thread into tasks.” Capture from chat, email, and Slack is real, and it is user-triggered except where GitHub events update linked tasks.
- Task IDs exist (they are embedded in GitHub branch names). That is a software-tracker primitive BrownBoard does not have.
- Docs are a first-class object inside the PM tool. Roadmaps and sprints are first-class. A separate Goals view was not found.

### Gaps
- No primary page found for a meetings or Zoom product that transcribes calls into tasks. Absence is based on the integrations index, features index, and the 2024 founder comment, not on an exhaustive help-center crawl.
- Exact tier checkmarks for Notion, email, and calendar were stripped in the pricing fetch.
- No date was captured for the Product Hunt “#1 Product of the Day” badge. Feature pages still carry a stale “Dart launched on Product Hunt today” banner.
- The February 2024 Vue/Django/OpenAI stack was not reconfirmed on current engineering pages. It may be outdated after the agent-era rewrite.
- “100,000 teams” is a homepage claim. YC still says “thousands of teams.” No independent count was found.

Dart sells to small and cross-functional teams that want AI to do project admin, with a free cap of four teammates and paid seats at $8 or $12 per teammate per month on the yearly prices shown. The company is a 10-person, still-active YC W22 startup in San Francisco. A public dollar amount for funding was not available.

## What are Dart’s greatest strengths?

### Takeaway
The strengths that show up in both the product and user comments are AI that turns a sentence into a groomed task tree, a fast keyboard-first PM UI, a wide structured-work surface (board, list, calendar, roadmap, docs, sprints), and, as of 2026, a place to point coding and other agents at those tasks.

### Cited Findings
- AI task admin is the original product: chat or a title produces tasks, subtasks, filled properties, duplicate detection, and reports. Founders’ example workflows were changelog drafts from completed work and splitting a PRD into subtasks. — [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=39442368); [Features FAQ](https://www.dartai.com/features)
- Named customers on Dart’s own pages describe that loop in concrete terms. Ahmad Taleb, product manager: a ChatGPT plan for 90 days of an MVP was created as tasks in the workspace, “with scary accuracy.” A verified customer: jotting a task takes as long as a notebook note, and Dart also creates subtasks, categories, and deadlines. Sijia Qiu, analytics consultant: it replaced other kanban apps because of auto-filled properties and generated subtasks. Alex W Castro, team lead: AI “takes the work out of project management” and fixed an adoption problem. Elina Kokko, IT project manager: set up in minutes with recurring weekly, monthly, and yearly sprint tasks. — [Homepage testimonials](https://www.dartai.com/); [Board layout testimonials](https://www.dartai.com/features/board-layout)
- Speed and low setup cost are the other repeated praise. Craig Bonnoit, CTO: “10x faster and cleaner.” A longtime Asana user: first PM tool they could use without reading docs. David Paffenholz, CEO: every feature via keyboard shortcuts, customizable layout, work and personal tasks separated. Andre Cobham, CMO: fast load time and shortcuts. Capterra review, 9 June 2026, Mary Ruth J., marketing assistant: creating and updating tasks “takes almost no effort”; AI helped brainstorming. — [dartai.com](https://www.dartai.com/); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10018804/Dart/)
- The 2026 product adds agent execution on top of that admin layer: assign a task to Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, Devin, or a custom agent; keep the spec, status, assignee, and subtasks on the task; Chat mode and Build mode stay linked. — [itsdart.com](https://itsdart.com/)
- Breadth versus a single board: docs, roadmaps, sprints, dependencies, time tracking, multiple task types, forms, and saved views ship in the same workspace. Desktop apps on Mac, Windows, and Linux, plus iOS and Android, dark and light mode. — [Pricing](https://www.dartai.com/pricing)
- Capture paths that reduce retyping: Slack message to task, email forward to task, GitHub branch/PR status mapping, ChatGPT and Claude on the free tier, MCP. — [Slack](https://www.dartai.com/integrations/slack); [Email](https://www.dartai.com/integrations/email); [GitHub](https://www.dartai.com/integrations/github); [Pricing](https://www.dartai.com/pricing)
- Price as a strength for small teams: $0 up to four people, then $8 per teammate per month on the yearly figure, with unlimited tasks, docs, and storage claimed on every tier. Dart’s comparison blog claims setup “under 1 day” versus Jira “2–4 weeks,” and “15+ key integrations” versus Jira’s “3,000+” apps. Those comparison numbers are vendor marketing. — [Pricing](https://www.dartai.com/pricing); [Dart blog](https://www.dartai.com/blog/jira-vs-wrike-vs-dart-which-collaboration-tool-should-you-use)
- Security claims, relevant because AI touches task content: SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, CCPA, TLS 1.2 in transit, AES-256 at rest, Google SSO or email codes, role-based access, and “your data is not stored or used to train models by our AI providers.” SSO enablement is Business-only. These are vendor statements; the certificate was not independently checked. — [Security](https://www.dartai.com/security)

### Inferences
- The strongest evidence is qualitative and first-party (testimonials, founder demos) plus a handful of 2026 Capterra reviews. The pattern is consistent: people who hate ticket grooming like Dart. That is a different strength from “best issue tracker” or “best whiteboard.”
- Agent orchestration is the current strategic bet. The older, better-documented strength is still AI grooming of human tasks. Both are in the product; the grooming loop has more user quotes.

### Gaps
- No independent time-and-motion study of the “seven hours a week” claim. See weaknesses for how that number conflicts with the founders’ own 2024 figure.
- Testimonial titles and employers are as published by Dart. They were not verified against LinkedIn or similar.
- SOC 2 Type II was not confirmed via a trust-report PDF in this pass. The security page asserts it.

## How is Dart different from BrownBoard?

### Takeaway
Dart is an AI-native task system: chat, email, and Slack become structured work items, and agents can be assigned to execute them. BrownBoard is a shared visual room: the same tasks appear as a kanban and as cards on a drawing canvas. They overlap only at “a team looking at a board.”

### Cited Findings
- Dart’s board is a kanban of task cards with status columns, drag-and-drop, and AI-filled properties. Layouts beside it are list, calendar, and roadmap, plus docs and sprints. — [Board layout](https://www.dartai.com/features/board-layout); [Pricing](https://www.dartai.com/pricing)
- Dart’s collaboration model is a workspace of teammates, admins, and guests (guest and admin roles are Premium), with role-based access, comments, notifications, and live sync of task records. Agents are first-class assignees. — [Pricing](https://www.dartai.com/pricing); [itsdart.com](https://itsdart.com/); [Security](https://www.dartai.com/security)
- Dart has task IDs used in GitHub branch names, task types (epic, issue, story, bug, milestone), dependencies, sprints, time tracking, and an AI chat that creates and updates that structure. — [GitHub](https://www.dartai.com/integrations/github); [Pricing](https://www.dartai.com/pricing)
- BrownBoard, per the comparison brief: shared rooms opened by link; guests may join; signed-in accounts can name, save, claim, and own rooms. Two views of one room: kanban and a freeform whiteboard. Kanban tasks have title, description, status, urgency, category, images, and position; categories are the columns. The whiteboard is a drawing canvas (pen, shapes, text, sticky notes, images, connectors, and task cards linked back to the kanban). Realtime sync via Supabase. Web app plus an Electron shell. Dark mode. It does not ship cycles or sprints, GitHub or PR sync, issue IDs, roadmaps, a docs wiki, an enterprise admin suite, or an AI agent that creates or assigns tasks.
- No Dart features page reviewed describes a drawing surface, sticky notes, connectors, or a freeform canvas. The word “whiteboard” does not appear in the product pages fetched. Design help is an AI design agent and “generate concepts and prototypes,” which is generation, not a shared sketching room. — [Features](https://www.dartai.com/features); [itsdart.com](https://itsdart.com/)
- Both products are realtime, have a dark theme, ship a desktop app, and show tasks as cards on a board. Dart’s desktop matrix is Mac, Windows, and Linux apps plus iOS and Android. BrownBoard’s desktop shell is Electron. An App Store reviewer called the iOS Dart app “not a native app,” which is a packaging complaint, not evidence it is Electron. — [Pricing](https://www.dartai.com/pricing); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dart-project-management/id1614157527)

### Inferences
- Axis of difference: Dart captures and assigns structured work, including to agents. BrownBoard hosts a human conversation on a shared canvas that happens to contain the tasks.
- Dart is closer to Linear, Height, or an AI-skinned Asana than to a whiteboard product. BrownBoard is closer to a light Miro-plus-kanban room than to an issue tracker.
- A team that wants GitHub-linked issues, sprints, roadmaps, or an agent to open a PR is outside BrownBoard’s shipped product. A team that wants to sketch, stick notes, and drag task cards around a drawing is outside Dart’s documented product.

### Gaps
- Dart’s “live syncing” implementation (transport, guest model, conflict handling) was not compared technically to BrownBoard’s Supabase rooms. Only the product claims were available.
- Whether any Dart customer uses an embedded third-party whiteboard was not researched. Nothing in the first-party feature list suggests one.

## Where could they be confused, and where do they not compete?

### Takeaway
They can be confused only at the phrase “a kanban board for a small team, with a desktop app and dark mode.” They do not compete on AI agents, issue tracking, or on a shared drawing room. A separate naming hazard is the Dart language, and another product at dart.ceo.

### Cited Findings
- Dart’s own name for the kanban is “board layout” and “dartboards.” Cards move across columns. That is the surface someone could mistake for BrownBoard’s board view. — [Board layout](https://www.dartai.com/features/board-layout)
- Dart markets itself against Monday.com, ClickUp, Asana, spreadsheets, and Jira in testimonials and in a Jira/Wrike comparison, and it offers CSV import from Linear and Jira. Those are the competitors Dart chooses. BrownBoard is not among them. — [Homepage](https://www.dartai.com/); [Dart blog](https://www.dartai.com/blog/jira-vs-wrike-vs-dart-which-collaboration-tool-should-you-use); [Linear integration](https://www.dartai.com/integrations/linear)
- Search for this company is polluted by the Dart language and by Flutter. The PM product is identified by itsdart.com, dartai.com, Keeple, Inc., and YC W22. — [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/dart); [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/dart-08b6)
- dart.ceo is a different “Dart” (company brain, meeting summaries, $199 per month flat). It should not be cited as a feature of Keeple’s product. — [dart.ceo](https://dart.ceo/)

### Inferences
- Confusion with BrownBoard would be a category error made by someone who has only heard “visual task board.” Five minutes in either product breaks it: Dart asks you to chat a plan into issues and invite an agent; BrownBoard asks you to open a link and draw.
- They do not compete for engineering issue tracking (that is Dart vs Linear/Jira, and Dart is the thinner tracker). They do not compete for workshop facilitation or spatial planning (that is BrownBoard’s whiteboard; Dart has no equivalent).
- The narrow overlap is a very small group that only needs a shared kanban and does not care about agents or drawing. Even there, Dart’s free tier (four teammates, AI grooming, docs, sprints) is a much larger PM product than BrownBoard’s room, and BrownBoard’s guest-by-link model is a much lower-friction join than a teammate workspace.

### Gaps
- No third-party article was found that mentions BrownBoard and Dart together. The non-overlap is inferred from the two product descriptions, not from a market map.
- Public win/loss data (teams that evaluated both) does not exist in the sources reviewed, which is expected given BrownBoard’s early stage.
