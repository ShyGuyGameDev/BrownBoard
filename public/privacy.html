Chromatic

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Chromatic is a shared project board and whiteboard. This policy explains what information Chromatic collects when you use it at chromatic.vercel.app or in the Chromatic desktop app, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Account information

  • If you sign in with Google: your email address and the name on your Google account, which we use to create your account and pick your username. We only ask Google for your basic profile and email address. We never see your Google password, and we don't access your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google data.
  • If you sign up with email: your email address and a password. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider; we can't read them.
  • Profile details you choose to add: your username, a profile picture and a highlight colour.
  • Guests: if you join a room without an account, you get a temporary anonymous session and a random display name. No email or personal details are collected.

Content you create

Everything you add to a room is stored so you and your teammates can see it: room names, lists, cards, descriptions, checklists, labels, due dates, comments, drawings and items on the canvas, and files or images you upload.

Activity in a room

While you're in a room, other people in it can see that you're online, your name and colour, what you have selected or are editing, and where you are on the board or canvas. This is sent live and isn't kept after you leave. Cards also keep a history of changes (for example "moved to Done") so teammates can see what happened.

Usage and device information

  • Analytics: we use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages are used. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify you personally. We also record anonymous visit statistics in our own database: when a visit started, how long it lasted, how many screens were opened, whether it started on the home page or in a room, the screen size category and the browser language. These aren't linked to your account, rooms or IP address, use no browser storage, and are deleted after about 90 days. They aren't recorded if your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. We may publish overall totals.
  • Your browser's storage: Chromatic saves a few preferences on your device, such as your theme, board filters and the room to return to after signing in, plus your sign-in session. These stay on your device and aren't used for tracking or advertising.
  • Server logs: our hosting and database providers keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

How we use your information

  • To provide Chromatic: sign you in, save your rooms and show them to the people you share them with.
  • To show your name, picture and live activity to others in the rooms you join.
  • To send account emails you ask for, such as confirming your email or resetting your password.
  • To keep Chromatic secure, fix problems and understand how it's used so we can improve it.

We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use your content or Google account data to train AI models.

Who can see your information

  • People in your rooms. Anyone you share a room with, or who joins with its link, can see the room's content and the names, pictures and activity of the people in it.
  • Service providers who run Chromatic for us and may only use your data to do so: Supabase (accounts, database, file storage and live updates), Vercel (website hosting and analytics) and, if you use it, Google (sign-in).
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

Google user data

Chromatic's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use your Google email and name only to sign you in and set up your Chromatic account, and we don't transfer them to anyone except as needed to run Chromatic, comply with the law, or with your permission.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and content are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Rooms that were never named (for example, a guest's quick room) aren't kept.
  • Archived cards and lists are kept until someone in the room deletes them.
  • Backups and logs held by our providers are removed on their normal schedules.

Your choices

  • Edit your username, email, picture and colour in your account settings.
  • Delete cards, lists, files and rooms you own at any time.
  • Delete your account from your account settings. This deletes your profile, the rooms you own and your uploaded pictures, and removes you from other rooms. Comments you left in rooms you don't own may remain, without your profile.
  • Disconnect Google at any time from your Google Account connections.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing the address below.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to rooms is enforced by the database so only members can read them. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Chromatic isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we'll change the date at the top, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app.
